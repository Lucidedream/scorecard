Slope Strategy

Ball below feet - fade bias
• Ball centered
• Wider stance, more knee flex
• Stay low through the swing
• Clubface points right; expect fade, so aim left
• Prioritize posture and balance

Ball above feet - draw bias
• Ball centered; choke down
• Stand slightly taller
• Lie creates a shallower swing and left/draw bias, so aim right
• Feel a smooth inside-out path; a steep outside-in move risks a heavy strike

Downhill lie - low/fade bias
• Ball slightly back
• Match shoulders and body to the slope, lead side lower
• Slope delofts the club; expect a lower launch
• Swing down the slope with a controlled fade pattern, so aim left
• Don't force an inside-out path; it risks a heavy strike

Uphill lie - high/draw bias
• Ball slightly forward
• Match shoulders and body to the slope, trail side lower
• Swing up the slope with a shallow, inside-out pattern
• Expect higher flight and less carry, so aim right and take enough club

Downhill + ball below feet - strongest low fade
• Ball slightly back; wider stance and extra knee flex
• Match the downhill slope and stay low
• Controlled, slope-following fade swing
• Expect low flight moving right, so aim clearly left

Downhill + ball above feet - biases partly cancel
• Ball slightly back; choke down and stand slightly taller
• Match shoulders and body to the downhill slope
• Expect low flight with modest curve
• Aim near target, adjusting for whichever slope is stronger

Uphill + ball below feet - biases partly cancel
• Ball slightly forward; wider stance and more knee flex
• Match the uphill slope and stay low
• Expect high flight with limited curve
• Aim near target or slightly left; prioritize balance

Uphill + ball above feet - strongest high draw
• Ball slightly forward; choke down and stand slightly taller
• Match shoulders and body to the uphill slope
• Make a shallow, inside-out, slope-following swing
• Expect a high draw, possibly a hook, so aim clearly right

Universal rule
• Match your body to the slope
• Accept the lie's natural shot shape
• Choose balance and contact over speed
