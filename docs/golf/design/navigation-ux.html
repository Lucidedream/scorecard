Show what exists, hide what doesn't

Four changes to how you move through the app: a horizontal main menu, a course list that earns its vertical space, a setup screen whose Back button stops teeing off, and player pickers that stop advertising players who have never played.

§ 1Main menu

Three tiles, not three rows

The menu has exactly three destinations and never grows. A vertical list is built for an unknown number of items; with three fixed ones it wastes the screen and makes you scroll past two things to reach the third.

Three tiles across the top show all options at once, and ◂ ▸ moves between them. Nothing is ever hidden, and every destination is one Confirm away.

§ 2Courses

Vertical is right; the rows were just thin

You said the vertical list works because you can see every course at once — agreed, and the list stays. What it lacked was anything to look at: a course was a bare name on a row.

Each row now carries what you actually choose between — par, hole count and tees — so you can pick without opening anything.

§ 3Leaving setup

A Back button that starts a round is the actual bug

On the roster, Back currently tees off. That is why it reads as confusing — not because of its label, but because it is the only Back in the app that goes forwards.

You suggested renaming it Complete. That fixes the wording, but it leaves one button doing two unrelated jobs, and a button called Complete that returns you to player selection would be its own puzzle. The cleaner split, which is also what your "maybe I'll add a short press as confirm" instinct points at:

ButtonDoesWhy
BackReturns to the player count Back means back, on every screen, with no exception here
ConfirmStarts the round → hole 1 Labelled Start, so the button says what happens

Why not "Complete"

Every player on the roster is already playable — §16.3 made it a review step, not a form. So there is nothing to complete; there is only "go back" and "start". Naming the two actions after what they do needs no new vocabulary.

If you would still rather have the word, say so and it becomes the Confirm label instead of Start — but it should be on the button that finishes setup, not the one that retreats from it.

§ 4Empty players

Don't advertise players who have never played

History and Trends both open on a four-row picker, with every slot listed and the empty ones marked No rounds. On a device only you have used, three of the four rows are permanent dead ends.

The data to fix this already exists: publishPlayers() builds a presentMask from the index and then draws all four rows anyway, disabling the absent ones. Only emit rows for present slots.

The case this must not break

If nobody has rounds — a fresh device, or an index that failed to load — filtering leaves an empty list. That needs a real empty state saying so, not a blank screen. It also must stay distinguishable from the load-failure case, which already has its own message: "no rounds yet" and "couldn't read your rounds" call for different reactions from you.

Mockups render at true 480 × 800 in pure black and white, matching the panel. Figures use the owner's real courses and round counts.

Glyphs in the tile row are placeholders for whatever the icon set provides; the layout does not depend on them.
