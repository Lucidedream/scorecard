Golf scorecard · v8 catalog · design for review

Course picker in the main menu’s blocks

New round and Course Map share one picker. Today its levels are plain lists. This proposal rebuilds them from the main menu’s vocabulary: bordered blocks, the focused block drawn inverted, a big figure where a number matters, and a detail box that explains the focused block.

Panels are 1:1 at the X4’s 480 × 800 portrait size. Course names are real; ratings, review counts, category scores and region counts are invented for illustration. The Recent names are your own courses, shown as examples.

1 · Hub: three tiles and a detail box

New round and Course Map share it · only the header differs

This is the main menu’s shape. The three ways in are always visible, one Confirm away, and the box below says what the focused tile holds.

  • Recent previews its courses in the box. Open shows them as cards (screen 3’s card style) so you can pick one.
  • My courses says how many are on the card. By region says “425 courses · 31 regions · 249 with hole maps”.
  • On the hub the Confirm cell reads Open. Inside New round, a course card’s Confirm reads Start, as §22.1 already does.
  • Buttons: Left/Right or the side rocker move between tiles. Touch: tap a tile.

Icons are Lucide history, flag and map-pin, added to the existing icon manifest. When the card has no catalog, By region is hidden and the strip becomes two tiles.

2 · Regions: a grid of blocks

31 regions · 10 per page · 4 pages

Each region is a block with its name and course count as a big figure, so you can see at a glance where the courses are.

  • Every button steps through the blocks in reading order. Left/Right and the rocker move one block, wrapping onto the next page. Touch: tap a block, swipe to turn the page.
  • Memory is unchanged: regions.txt is already held in RAM (≤64 × 32 B), and the grid only draws the visible ten.
Decide: order regions by course count, as shown, so the provinces you are likely to want come first? Or A–Z, as in the index file? Count order needs no file change; the device sorts 31 entries.

3 · Courses: cards with the rating as the figure

Region courses · also the card style for Recent and My courses

Each course is a full-width card: the name in large type, holes, par and map availability below it, and the rating as the big figure on the right with its review count underneath. The figure sits where par sits on today’s course rows (§22.2).

  • The sort is a two-block segment at the top. The current sort is underlined, and Confirm on the segment flips it. On touch, tap either half.
  • Six cards fit on a page. The list scrolls in windows, as P2 does today, so RAM stays at one page of names.
  • In New round the Confirm cell reads Start; in Course Map it reads Open, which goes to Course info.
  • Unrated courses show “—” in place of the figure. In My courses, which has no ratings, the figure is par, as on today’s rows.

4 · Course info: action tiles above the facts

Course Map → a course · illustrative figures

The three things you can do with a course become a small tile strip: Play, Hole maps, Tee info. The facts sit below as a big rating figure, six category bars and a short fact line.

  • Left/Right and the rocker move between the tiles. Confirm’s label follows the tile: Start, Open, Open. Touch: tap a tile.
  • Play keeps the §41.5 rule: it is present exactly when New round is.
  • Owner courses with no baigolf facts keep the header and tiles, and the box shows holes, par, yards and nines only.
  • Today this screen is a list, so the facts are rows you scroll past. Here they are read-only content, and the only things you can select are the three tiles.

What stays the same

  • Every level, the Back behaviour, the return-to-same-row context and Play’s visibility rule from P2/P3.
  • Memory: still one page of names. Region and Recent data are already in RAM. No change to the catalog files.
  • The nine picker, player setup and scoring screens are not part of this redesign.

The screens are built from existing pieces: the tile strip and detail box from GolfHomeActivity (factored out as §34.3 already suggested) and the footer from GolfUiListActivity. The grid and the cards are the only new drawing code, and both are host-testable as pure layout functions in GolfUiLayout.h.
