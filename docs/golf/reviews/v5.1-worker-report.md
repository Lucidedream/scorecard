A maximum-length realistic 39-byte course name (`The International Golf Club of Shanghai`) measures 302 px and is shortened to `The International Gol...`, exactly 174 px; label + 8 px gap + battery/percentage therefore occupies 248 of the available 422 px, with a separate 8 px title gap reserved as well.

The longest detail is the New Round last-round sentence with a maximum-length course name. At the 418 px inner text width it wraps to two 29 px lines. The detail box changes from 552 px to 133 px in RoundedRaff (36 padding + 29 title + 10 gap + 58 body), and from 550 px to 139 px in Lyra (the same geometry with a 16 px gap).
